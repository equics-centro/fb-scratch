Scratch & Win Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what Equics collects when you play the Equics Scratch & Win promotion and how we use it.

What we collect

  • The name and mobile number you type into the form.
  • If you choose "Fill my name with Facebook", only your Facebook name, which we use to fill in the name box. This is optional, and we never receive your Facebook password.
  • The invoice number of your bill.
  • The prize you won and the date and time you played.

Why we collect it

  • To make sure each mobile number and each bill is used only once.
  • To check your bill and give you your prize, and to make sure each bill is used only once.
  • To contact you about this promotion.

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Where it is stored and for how long

Your information is stored securely in Google Firebase and can only be viewed by Equics staff. We keep it until the promotion ends and all prizes are claimed, and delete it within 90 days after that.

How to delete your data

To ask us to delete your information at any time, email admin@equics.mv with the subject "Delete my Scratch & Win data" and include the mobile number you used. We will delete your entry within 7 days and confirm by reply.

Contact
